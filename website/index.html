Good to know

Does Haven replace Windows?

No. Haven is a skin that sits on top of Windows. Your apps, files and settings are untouched.

Can I remove it?

Yes. Uninstall Haven from Windows Settings and your usual desktop returns.

Do the widgets need internet?

Battery, calendar and clock work offline. Weather and stocks need a connection to fetch fresh data.

Will my apps and games still work?

Yes. Apps and games you’ve installed open as normal. Haven changes how the desktop looks and feels, not what your software does.

Is Haven made by Apple?

No. Haven is independent software and has no connection with Apple Inc. Mac and macOS are trademarks of Apple Inc.

Where is my data kept?

Your layout, theme and settings stay on your PC. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Haven is built to run on your PC. This page explains what it stores, what it sends over the internet and the choices you have.

What stays on your PC

Haven keeps your settings on your device: theme, wallpaper, widget layout, dock items and chosen city. You don’t need an account to use Haven.

What Haven reads on your PC

  • Battery level, time and date, for the battery, clock and calendar widgets.
  • The track currently playing in Spotify, for the music widget and menu bar. This is read on your device from Windows.
  • The apps you add to the dock.

What Haven sends over the internet

  • Weather: the location you choose is sent to Open-Meteo to get a forecast.
  • Stocks: the ticker symbols you follow are sent to a market data provider to get prices.
  • Updates: Haven may check for new versions.

These requests are needed for those features to work. Turn the widgets off and Haven makes no such requests.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your personal data, show you ads or build a profile of you.

Third parties
